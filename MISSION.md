# Flat Email exists to return email to its owner

Our mission is to make personal and organisational mail fully portable, searchable, durable, and independent of proprietary platforms by transforming messages, attachments, metadata, labels, and threads into plain files that anyone can inspect, back up, move, index, and use with the tools they already trust.

_We believe email should not be trapped inside opaque services. It should be an open, ownable archive: readable from disk, useful offline, compatible with automation, and resilient for decades._
